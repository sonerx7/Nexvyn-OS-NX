Neon Drift audio licenses
=========================

Every audio file in this folder (music/*.ogg and sfx/*.wav) was synthesized
by tools/neondrift_audio.py for this game. The generator uses only numpy and
the Python standard library (ffmpeg/libvorbis encodes the music). No samples,
recordings or third-party audio were used.
