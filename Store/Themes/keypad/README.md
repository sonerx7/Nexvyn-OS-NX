## Install

**Store:** App Market > Themes > Keypad > Get, then Apply. **File:** open `keypad.nxvtheme` in Files and choose Install and Apply.

Themes are managed in *System Settings > Appearance > Themes* (Undo, Restore Defaults). Everything in this pack is original: the wallpaper is drawn by a script, the pixel icons are drawn for this theme by a script and the sounds are synthesized.

Bu paketteki her şey özgündür: duvar kâğıdı bir script ile çizilir, piksel simgeler bu tema için script ile çizilir, sesler sentezlenir.

Format: Nexvyn OS `docs/NXVAPP_FORMAT.md`, section 9.
