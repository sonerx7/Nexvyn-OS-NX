## Install

**Store:** App Market > Themes > Pocket > Get, then Apply. **File:** open `pocket.nxvtheme` in Files and choose Install and Apply.

Themes are managed in *System Settings > Appearance > Themes* (Undo, Restore Defaults). Everything in this pack is original: the wallpaper is drawn by a script, the glossy 16-colour icons are drawn for this theme by a script and the sounds are synthesized.

Bu paketteki her şey özgündür: duvar kâğıdı bir script ile çizilir, parlak 16 renkli simgeler bu tema için script ile çizilir, sesler sentezlenir.

Format: Nexvyn OS `docs/NXVAPP_FORMAT.md`, section 9.
